Paragraph Formatting

The Increase Indent and Decrease Indent buttons ( and ) change the levels of list items:

  • Color Types (Level 1 paragraph/ list item)
    • Red (Level 2)
      • Rose (Level 3)
      • Cherry
      • Wine
    • Green
      • Olive
      • Forest
      • Jade
  • Shirt Types (Level 1)
    • T-shirt (Level 2)
    • Blouse
    • Smoke
    • Sweater
