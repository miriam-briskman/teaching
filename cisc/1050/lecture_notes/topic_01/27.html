Operating Systems

On Mac, use the following short guide to detect its version: https://support.apple.com/en-us/109033

For Chromebooks, use the following short guide: https://support.google.com/chromebook/answer/177889?hl=en

For a Linux computer, use the Stack Exchange answer here: https://unix.stackexchange.com/a/88647

New! Regardless of your computer type, you can use the https://whatsmyos.com/ website to find the name and version of your operating system.

  • Once you open this webpage in a browser of your device, the operating system information will be displayed at the top of the screen.
