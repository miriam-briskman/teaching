Advanced Searches

Keywords and Special Symbols

Besides just searching for a pure set of words, you can refine the search using a few techniques that involve special keywords or symbols:

Keyword / Symbol Purpose Example
AND Results containing both items pizza AND cola
OR Results containing either or both/all items England OR Ireland
NOT Results excluding the phrase/word that follows college NOT community
- (minus sign) Results excluding the phrase/word that follows college -community
" " (quotations) Results containing exactly this phrase "red jelly beans"
* (asterisk) Results with various characters in place of * *th grade exams
() (parentheses) Results with imposed order (red OR sore) AND gums
