Browsers

Examples of popular browsers include:

Browser Name Creator Logo Download & Info Link
Google Chrome Google   https://www.google.com/chrome/
Mozilla Firefox Mozilla Foundation or https://www.mozilla.org/en-US/
Microsoft Edge Microsoft  or https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/edge
Safari Apple   https://www.apple.com/safari/
Opera Opera   https://www.opera.com/
  • Microsoft Edge is shipped with the Windows operating system. In other words, it isn’t needed to be installed.
  • Safari is shipped with the Mac operating system. You can also find it on your other Apple devices: iPhones, iPads, etc.
  • Google Chrome, Mozilla Firefox, or Opera might be already installed on your computer or on your organization’s computers. If they aren’t installed, use the links in the table above to download them to your computer, regardless of what operating system it has.
