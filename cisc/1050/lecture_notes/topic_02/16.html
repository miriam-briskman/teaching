Searching Images

Browsers allow you to search for images, and search with images!

On Google, you can:

  • Search for an item, and then click the “Images” tab to see all detected images.
  • Click on the small image icon: to search the Internet using an image:
    • By pasting the URL of an image, or
    • By uploading an image.

Google will then ‘translate’ the image into words and search them!
